01 / Public context

Built for Rosewood's current digital direction

Rosewood's October 2025 brand relaunch describes a redesigned, discovery-led digital experience that brings editorial storytelling and transactions together across rooms, dining, spa and experiences. This case uses that public direction as the business context — not as evidence of an internal problem.

Business hypothesis

Preserve valid guest context as a traveller moves from inspiration into reservation and then into stay curation.

BA outcome

Make requirements testable, dependencies explicit, content governable and UAT traceable before build reaches release.

Guardrail

No proprietary data, no claimed conversion uplift, and no assumption that a historically reported vendor is the current production stack.

Why this is a BA case: the portfolio focuses on discovery, requirements, business rules, stories, acceptance criteria, dependencies, UAT and release readiness — not on speculative visual redesign.

03 / User journey

Design the handoffs, not just the screens

DiscoverEditorial, destination and property inspiration
ConsiderRooms, offers and experience context
ConfigureDates, party, preferences
SelectRoom and rate choice
CheckoutGuest details, consent, payment
ConfirmReservation confidence and support path
Curate stayDining, wellness and experiences
To-be principle: do not make the guest repeatedly reconstruct intent that the experience already knows. Preserve context where permitted, keep it editable, and degrade gracefully when integrations fail.

05 / UAT & release

Trace business intent all the way to acceptance

Critical UAT

Context handoff, consent, manual fallback, AEM validation, mobile preview, integration timeout and analytics.

Defect severity

P1 includes booking blocked, wrong reservation context, privacy/security risk or materially incorrect price/policy display.

Release gate

No unresolved P1; critical UAT executed; content, integrations, analytics and operations all have named owners.

06 / Measurement

Measure the journey without inventing the result

Conversion

Discovery-to-booking start rate; booking completion rate.

Experience

Post-booking engagement with dining, wellness and experiences.

Quality

Content defect rate; UAT pass rate; recovery success after transient failures.

Targets are intentionally expressed as direction-of-travel until baseline Rosewood data is available.
