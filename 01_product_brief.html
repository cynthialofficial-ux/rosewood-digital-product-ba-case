Public context

Rosewood’s October 2025 brand relaunch described a digital experience that combines editorial discovery with seamless transaction, enabling guests to explore and book rooms, dining, spa treatments and experiences. The Business Analyst role sits within the global Digital Platforms and Experience team, reporting to the Manager, Digital Product, Global.

Case principle: this is an independent portfolio case based on public information. It demonstrates BA method; it does not claim access to Rosewood’s private architecture, data or roadmap.

Opportunity hypothesis

A discovery-led site creates a valuable BA challenge: preserve context and intent as users move between content, booking, profile/consent and property experiences.

This case therefore models a feature set called Discovery-to-Booking Continuity.

Goal

Make the transition from inspiration to reservation feel continuous and premium, while ensuring requirements are testable, integrations are explicit, content can be governed globally and property operations remain ready to fulfil the experience.

In scope

  1. Context handoff from Brand.com discovery content into room booking.
  2. Persistence of property, dates and party information where technically valid.
  3. Post-booking discovery of dining, wellness and experience options.
  4. Permission-based guest-profile / consent handoff.
  5. AEM content requirements and content-quality gates.
  6. Analytics events for the discovery-to-booking funnel.
  7. Error recovery and service-quality requirements.
  8. UAT, release readiness and business-user enablement.

Out of scope

  • Replacing the current CRS / booking engine.
  • Redesigning pricing or revenue-management rules.
  • Defining current production CRM architecture.
  • Claiming measured conversion uplift without Rosewood data.

Success measures

Booking funnel
Booking-start rate and booking completion rate.
Experience
Post-booking engagement with dining, wellness and experiences.
Release quality
Critical content defects, UAT pass rate, unresolved P1/P2 defects.
Resilience
Recovery success after transient integration failures.
